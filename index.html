Privacy Policy — DentVerify Pro

Effective date: November 5, 2026
Extension version: 3.9.4

1. What the extension does

DentVerify Pro helps users manage reusable dental insurance benefit-plan templates on supported websites. Features include detecting supported form fields, previewing and correcting values, saving and searching templates, applying templates to forms, managing plan URLs, importing and exporting backups, and finding or selecting matching eligibility verification (EV) count items. These actions are initiated by the user.

2. Information accessed and processed

When a user requests a preview, save, apply, or search action, the extension may read supported form labels, values, dropdown selections, employer names, and relevant visible webpage text from the active website. Users may edit detected values before saving. EV searches may temporarily examine procedure codes, keywords, names, or identifiers visible on the active webpage to locate matching items. Such search content is used for the requested matching action and is not intended to be saved as a plan template.

The extension processes dental insurance benefit information, which may include coverage, deductibles, maximums, and procedure-related details. Because supported websites may also display patient or insurance information, the extension could encounter personal or sensitive information during a user-initiated action.

3. Information stored

The extension stores selected reusable plan template fields and related metadata locally, which may include employer or custom plan titles, categories, favorites, dates, benefit values, user-edited values, plan-level comments, and saved page URLs. It also stores local preferences and owner access information, including the username, salted cryptographic hashes of the owner password and PIN, and session expiration information. Plaintext passwords and PINs are not intended to be stored.

4. Patient information and review responsibilities

The extension includes filtering intended to exclude patient-identifying or encounter-specific fields from saved templates. It is designed to store reusable plan information, not patient records. Filtering cannot guarantee that every sensitive field or user-entered comment will be excluded on every website. Users must inspect previews, saved fields, comments, and exported backups and must not save patient names, dates of birth, medical record numbers, subscriber identifiers, or other patient-identifying information in reusable templates. The extension is not a patient-record system.

5. How information is used

Information is used to preview and correct detected values, organize and search templates, populate supported form fields, find and select matching EV entries, manage local owner access, and perform user-requested operations such as copying or opening URLs and importing or exporting backups. It is not used for advertising, creditworthiness decisions, or lending.

6. Local storage and retention

Saved templates, URLs, settings, and owner authentication records are stored in the current Chrome browser profile using chrome.storage.local. Core template features do not require a developer-operated cloud account or synchronization service. Locally stored information generally remains until a user deletes it, clears extension storage, or removes the extension and its stored data. Exported JSON files are saved to a user-selected or browser-configured location and remain outside extension-managed storage until the user deletes them.

7. Sharing and transfers

The extension is not designed to sell user data or automatically send saved templates or owner authentication records to the developer, advertisers, analytics providers, or an extension-operated server. When users apply data to a third-party website, that website may process the entered information according to its own privacy practices. User-requested clipboard, URL-opening, export, and import actions are handled through the browser and the destinations selected by the user.

8. Chrome permissions

activeTab and scripting allow user-initiated interaction with the active webpage, including reading supported fields, applying values, and selecting matching EV items. storage saves local templates and settings. tabs opens saved URLs at the user's request. clipboardWrite copies URLs when requested. downloads supports user-requested backup exports.

9. Security and user controls

Owner passwords and PINs are stored as salted cryptographic hashes rather than plaintext. Users can manage or delete saved plans and export backups. Anyone with access to the device, browser profile, or exported files may be able to access saved information. Users should protect their Chrome profiles, credentials, and backups. No security control guarantees absolute protection.

10. Children

This extension is intended for adult professional users and is not directed to children.
